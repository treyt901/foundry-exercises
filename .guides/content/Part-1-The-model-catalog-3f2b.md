# Part 1 — Explore the model catalog

Azure AI Foundry's **model catalog** is where you discover, compare, and deploy
AI models — from OpenAI's GPT family to open models from Meta, Mistral AI,
Microsoft, Cohere, and many others.

### Open the catalog

1. In a **new browser tab**, go to [https://ai.azure.com](https://ai.azure.com)
   and sign in.
2. Find the **Model catalog** (in the left navigation, or under *Explore*).
   You can also go straight to
   [https://ai.azure.com/explore/models](https://ai.azure.com/explore/models).

> 💡 Keep that tab open — **the quiz on the next page can only be answered by
> exploring the catalog.** None of the answers are on this page.

### What to explore (5–10 minutes)

While you browse, make sure you try each of these:

- **Search** for a model by name (try `Phi-4`, `Llama`, `whisper`,
  `text-embedding-3-small`).
- **Filters** — narrow the catalog by:
  - **Collections / publisher** — who made the model?
  - **Inference task** — what kind of work does it do? (chat completion,
    embeddings, text-to-image, speech recognition, …)
  - **Deployment options** — how can it be hosted?
- **Model cards** — click any model and skim its card. Notice what a card
  tells you: a description of what the model is good at, its publisher, its
  versions, and the ways it can be deployed.

### Questions to keep in mind

As you explore, try to answer these for yourself — the quiz will check:

- Who publishes which model families?
- If someone told you a *job to be done* (transcribe audio, generate an image,
  power a chatbot, build semantic search), could you pick a model for it?
- What's the difference between a **serverless (pay-as-you-go)** deployment
  and **managed compute**?

When you've had a good look around, move on to the quiz. ➡️
